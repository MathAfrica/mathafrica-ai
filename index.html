📚 Fractions

What is a fraction?

A fraction represents part of a whole.

Imagine a cake divided into 8 equal pieces. If you eat 3 pieces, you have eaten:

3/8

3 is the numerator — the number of pieces taken.

8 is the denominator — the total number of equal pieces.

✏️ Practice Question

A pizza is divided into 8 equal pieces. A child eats 3 pieces. What fraction of the pizza did the child eat?
